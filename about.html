Concept rationale

Built around what OBO already has.

The goal of this demo is not to redesign Online Biz Offers. It is to demonstrate how a small interactive layer could help visitors navigate an already broad content and resource ecosystem.

Why this test

OBO already separates resources across online business, marketing, AI tools, content creation, lead generation, affiliate marketing and SEO. A visitor may understand their problem before they understand which site category contains the answer.

This concept reverses that order: start with the visitor's intent, then route into the existing OBO structure.

1
Preserve the ecosystemExisting articles, resource hubs and store products remain the destinations.
2
Keep the first test smallThree questions are enough to test whether guided discovery changes behavior.
3
Make every route explainableRecommendations should have a clear connection to the visitor's stated goal.
4
Measure before expandingTrack starts, completions and downstream clicks before adding complexity.
