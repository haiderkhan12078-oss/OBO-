Services layer concept

From learning to the right action.

A recommendation layer can also identify when a visitor should keep learning versus when a service, resource or store offer is the more useful next step.

For readers

Content-first path

Keep visitors in education mode when they are still exploring or building understanding.

  • Recommended article or guide
  • Supporting resource hub
  • Optional newsletter or free resource CTA
  • No forced sales jump
See content paths
For higher intent

Action-first path

When a visitor signals a clearer need, route them toward a service or relevant implementation resource.

  • Goal-qualified service route
  • Relevant digital product
  • Context preserved from prior choices
  • Trackable CTA click

What the prototype proves

A layer, not a replacement.

The concept is deliberately modular. It could live inside a resource hub, article footer, sidebar CTA or landing page without rebuilding the whole site.

Article context

Pre-select or prioritize options based on the category a reader is already in.

Resource hub

Use the navigator as a front door when the resource library becomes too broad to scan manually.

Offer bridge

Show a product or service only when it logically follows from the visitor's stated intent.
